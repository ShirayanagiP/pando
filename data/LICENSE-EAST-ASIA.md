# East Asia boundary provenance and modifications

This is a local, generalized drawing map, assembled on 2026-10-20. Its **3,083 editable source units** are not a claim about today's official administrative totals or territorial sovereignty. Each country's source and license remain separate. Bundled originals and normalized GeoJSON are in `data/external/`; South and North Korean sources remain in `data/`.

## China

- Pinned source: [cn-atlas commit 6e83a19923e39f2c0e58a0a7ad29b349b2a71b9f](https://github.com/BarbarossaWang/cn-atlas/tree/6e83a19923e39f2c0e58a0a7ad29b349b2a71b9f), `prefectures.json`.
- Upstream license evidence: [contemporaneous MIT license](https://github.com/ruiduobao/shengshixian.com/blob/5290747be6fb5dd08443b0e382f6d986dc60d2cf/LICENSE). The license, redistribution package, hashes and detailed normalization are preserved in `external/china/provenance.json` and adjacent files.
- 333 prefecture-level divisions: 293 cities, 30 autonomous prefectures, 7 prefectures and 3 leagues. To cover the source extent, add 4 directly administered municipalities, 32 provincial-direct county-level units and Hong Kong/Macao as 2 whole units. These exceptions explain the 371 count. Taiwan's province-level source shape is removed in favor of the separate Taiwan layer.
- Sansha's source includes large outlines of reefs and submerged shoals. Only this unit is intersected with physical land from [Natural Earth 10m land](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-land/), the public-domain [vector bundle v5.1.2](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/v5.1.2/geojson/ne_10m_land.geojson). The result shows small dry-land shapes at their real scale; it does not enlarge reefs or display marine claim lines. `external/china/mask-sansha.py` reproduces this step from the bundled originals.

## Mongolia

- Continental mode: 330 soums plus 9 Ulaanbaatar districts. Source: National Statistics Office / OCHA ROAP, geoBoundaries MNG ADM2 pinned9469f09, represented year2021, CC BY3.0IGO. Native/English names and stableP-codes are from the NSO/OCHA source service. `external/mongolia/README-soums.md`, `soums-provenance.json` and `prepare-soums.py` retain attribution, hashes, name joins and modifications. New aimag outlines are dissolved from their soums; the old ADM1 boundaries are not used to cut the newer soums. Previously painted aimags pass their colors to their soums without changing legend names/colors.
- Macro mode retains 21 aimags plus Ulaanbaatar from the following 2017 source, with Korean display names and native/English search aliases:
- [Pinned geoBoundaries MNG ADM1 snapshot 9469f09](https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/tree/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/MNG/ADM1). Original metadata and GeoJSON are bundled in `external/mongolia/`.
- © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright), licensed under the [Open Database License 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/). This database's Mongolia-derived portions and modifications are available under ODbL 1.0; their reproducible source and preparation script are bundled. Other independently sourced country layers retain their own licenses.

## Russia

- Continental East Asia includes only the 21 federal subjects in the Far Eastern(11) and Siberian(10) federal districts; Buryatia and Zabaykalsky Krai use their post-2018 Far Eastern membership.
- 567 editing units: rayons, uluses, kozhuuns, urban okrugs and equivalent independently administered cities. Excluding cities would leave holes. Geometry is the pinned geoBoundaries RUS ADM2 OSM/Wambacher2017 source, not a claim that municipal boundaries have been updated through2026.
- © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL1.0. Source archive, stableOSMrelationIDs, provenance, name history, 21-subject membership and reproducible scripts are in `external/russia/README.md` and `provenance.json`. Russia-derived modified database portions remain available under ODbL; other country sources retain their own licenses.
- Before planar projection, negative longitudes are unwrapped by+360 so Chukotka and its islands stay continuous across180°. Raw geographic source coordinates remain unchanged. Kuril islands are not duplicated in the Japanese layer.
- Native/Korean/English names are provided for all567 units and their parent subjects.

The 926 towns/villages are grouped by prefecture, `N03_002` source subprefecture and `N03_003` county/branch, yielding 375 county groups and four Tokyo island branches. - The complete ward-separated source is `data/municipality/geojson/s0010/N03-21_210101.json` at the same pinned commit above, bundled as `municipalities-wards-source.geojson` (SHA-256 `cd4b4084f76401e98a9704ca7d75f77e5841029928ab360061e77f87d8bab502`). Use this source consistently for the entire Japanese coverage to avoid small differences between independently generalized city and ward outlines.

The ward-separated detailed layer, **1,896 valid source units**, remains in `micro-municipalities.geojson` for the future micro mode and is not loaded into the basic page. Official city/ward IDs retain their `JP-` code; aggregate IDs use a stable hash of prefecture/subprefecture/county. `prepare-basic-japan.py` reproduces both layers and each aggregate lists its member IDs.
- The source date remains **2021-01-01**; for example, its historical Hamamatsu seven-ward arrangement is preserved. Do not interpret the displayed counts as current administrative totals.
- Six source municipalities on Japanese-claimed, Russian-administered Southern Kuril islands are not editable municipalities in this map. Four uncoded, jurisdiction-undetermined source shapes are also excluded from editable counts. All ten originals are retained in `external/japan/context.geojson`; they have not been assigned invented municipal IDs. This is a display scope, not a resolution of the territorial dispute.

## Cartographic processing and compatibility

- Preserve the original South Korean IDs and projection, and existing North Korean IDs. Old saved projects default to the peninsula view. New IDs use separate `CN-`, `MN-`, `JP-` and `TW-` namespaces.
- Remove North/South source overlaps while retaining the South Korean boundary. Fill **8 enclosed gaps touching both source coverages** by extending adjacent northern units; when multiple northern regions contact a gap, split by nearest contacting boundary. `seam-repairs.json` records affected units, bounds and area. This corrects source misalignment for drawing, without claiming reconstruction of a legal border or adding administrative units.
- River/source-gap repair: enclosed holes between country sources (including China–North Korea and China–Mongolia) are filled to the nearest sampled contacting bank. Within a country, narrow gaps between administrative units are filled; broad reservoirs and single-unit water holes remain. Open river channels are closed only within one existing land component, within 2.5 map units of each bank and near a mapped river. Separate islands are not joined across straits. `water-gap-repairs.json` records the additions. Small unowned faces caused by rounding splits are assigned to one contacting bank on the shared 0.01 coordinate grid.
- The offline repair uses the public-domain Natural Earth [10m lakes/reservoirs](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-lakes/) and [rivers/lake centerlines](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-physical-vectors/10m-rivers-lake-centerlines/), pinned to the [v5.1.2 vector bundle](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/tree/v5.1.2/geojson). Features intersecting 70°E–150°E, 15°N–60°N are bundled under `external/hydrography/`, with original hashes and URLs in `provenance.json`. These masks protect existing lake water from additions; they do not cut new lake holes into previously filled administrative polygons. Uncatalogued broad inland water such as Yeongam reservoir is also retained. No hydrography is fetched or processed in the browser.
- Topology-preserving coverage simplification: China 1.0, Mongolia 0.4, Japan 0.7 and Taiwan 0.18 map units. Korean geometry is not simplified again. Coordinates are rounded to 0.01 map units, overlaps are noded once, shared edges are reused, and upper boundaries are dissolved from editable units. Project map units are a fixed planar visualization scale rather than equal-area measurements.
- Independent source overlaps use this display priority: South Korea, North Korea, Taiwan, Mongolia, Japan, China. In particular, a dedicated country/island dataset takes precedence over overlapping shapes in a broader source. Claims are not duplicated on top of each other. This convention does not imply an opinion about sovereignty.
- Native country names, selected Korean and English search aliases, viewport presets and source credits are added. Very small islands remain at their source scale; no decorative enlargement or sea labels are introduced. Existing source omissions and coastline generalization remain.
- `scripts/prepare-map.py` rebuilds the shared offline topology from bundled normalized files, using Shapely 2.1+. Country-specific preparation scripts remain beside their source files. No download or preparation is required during normal use. The expanded continental/world bundles are assembled by `scripts/build-levels.py` from the pinned six-country base and separately normalized new layers. The northern river/lake mask extent is recorded in `external/hydrography/north-provenance.json`. Russia is simplified at1.4 map units; its independently sourced seams are repaired on the shared precision grid.

- Final precision repair also closes sub-0.05-map-unit unowned faces between two contacting administrative banks after river-gap splitting and rounding. Known lakes and water holes retained by the source-gap pass are protected. Complete noded faces are assigned once to a contacting bank so a second rounded split cannot leave a dot-like hole; `continental-precision-repairs.json` records the changes. This fixes the tiny ring between Zavkhan Tosontsengel and Ikh-Uul without changing IDs or assignments.
- The continental Mongolia–China seam additionally recognizes nearly collinear contacts within half the output grid (0.005 map units), measured without rounding the tolerance buffer itself. A union-inserted vertex could otherwise make a real contacting bank appear disconnected and leave the soum/Inner Mongolia seam unfilled. Remaining noded faces touching both countries are closed even when a long, thin face exceeds the pinhole area limit. Tiny holes previously classified as single-source water are rechecked against the final banks. Known lakes, including existing Buir Lake water, and uncatalogued broad inland water remain protected. This correction is restricted to the Mongolia–China pair; source files, editable IDs, names, paint assignments and macro geometry are unchanged. Both repair logs are regenerated by `python scripts/build-levels.py continental`.
